## Data and purposes

The contact form stores your name, email, subject and message in the portal database to answer your inquiry. A hash of the IP address limits abuse. The basis is the controller's legitimate interest (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)); if the inquiry concerns a contract, pre-contractual steps (Article 6(1)(b)). Abuse prevention is based on legitimate interest.

A listing order includes buyer details, ad text and language versions, dates, package, contact details selected for publication, and payment and moderation information. The buyer's private email is not published automatically. Contract performance and pre-contractual steps rely on Article 6(1)(b); applicable accounting duties on Article 6(1)(c). Details marked public appear with an approved ad. Agreement to publish content is separate from the legal basis for necessary order processing.

The CMS stores administrator accounts, password hashes, sessions, editorial decisions and history to secure access under Article 6(1)(f). If an authorised administrator invokes AI, prompts and conversations may be stored. Manual ad translation may transmit ad text to Google Gemini when that feature is configured and used. Avoid unnecessary personal data in these features.

## Recipients and transfers

Cloudflare provides hosting, application functions and D1 storage. OVHcloud operates the kontakt@polacywbelgii.eu mailbox and receives messages sent to it and our replies. Stripe is involved only when payments are enabled; payments are not currently configured on the test environment. Google Gemini may be used if manual AI features are configured and invoked. Processor agreements, data locations and possible transfers outside the EEA still need the controller's verification; we make no unverified transfer assurances.

## Retention and rights

The approved operational rules provide for deletion of contact messages 365 days after a case is closed, AI history after 30 days, CMS sessions when they expire (within 8 hours), security logs after 90 days, and cancelled or rejected unpaid orders 365 days after case closure. Open cases, complaints, disputes, security incidents and legal obligations suspend deletion of the relevant data. Paid orders and transaction records are not automatically deleted until individual tax, accounting and claims obligations are established. The approved rule for manual backups requires encryption, restricted access and deletion within 30 days unless a specific legal obligation justifies longer retention. Automatic retention runs on TEST; the production schedule remains disabled pending migration and verification. sessionStorage data is removed when the tab closes.

You can request access, correction, erasure and restriction, and where applicable portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest. Contact us at the address above. You may complain to the competent data protection authority, including the Polish UODO or Belgian APD/GBA.

Providing contact form data is voluntary, but without the required fields we cannot receive and answer your message. The required order details are needed to process a listing order; without them you cannot submit it. A CMS administrator approves publication of listings. The portal does not make solely automated decisions about users that produce legal or similarly significant effects.
