1. The available packages are STANDARD, TOP and PREMIUM.

1. The customer chooses a package and period and provides the required details.

3. The customer must provide accurate information and material they are entitled to use.
4. Placing an order requires acceptance of the current terms. Every order is subject to moderation before publication.

1. Electronic payments are handled through Stripe. Available methods are shown during checkout.
2. A listing may be scheduled for display after payment is confirmed and the operator accepts it. Starting a payment does not mean that it has succeeded.
3. Sales documents are issued in accordance with applicable tax law.

## §5. Moderation

1. The operator may refuse content that is unlawful, contrary to these terms or outside the declared scope of the service.
2. Fraud, copyright infringement, promotion of illegal goods or services and other unlawful content are prohibited.
3. The operator informs the customer of a refusal and its reasons where required by law.
4. If a paid order is refused before display begins, the payment is refunded unless a lawful basis provides for another settlement.
5. Moderation decisions may be challenged in accordance with applicable law and the available procedure.

## §6. Display

1. A listing is displayed for the period in the accepted order and in the places assigned to its package.
2. After the period ends, it is no longer displayed as active.
3. Changes to listing content may require renewed moderation.
4. If display fails for reasons attributable to the operator, the customer has the remedies available under law, including performance or an appropriate settlement for the undelivered part.

1. A consumer generally has 14 days from conclusion of a distance service contract to withdraw, subject to statutory exceptions.
2. A withdrawal notice may be sent to kontakt@polacywbelgii.eu or the operator’s postal address.
3. If the consumer expressly requests the service to start before the withdrawal period ends, services provided before withdrawal are settled under applicable law.
4. Loss of the withdrawal right after full performance is possible only if the statutory conditions are met.
5. Detailed withdrawal information and a model withdrawal form must be supplied before the contract is concluded. These have not yet been finalised for this draft.

1. Send complaints to kontakt@polacywbelgii.eu with the order identifier and a description of the issue.
2. Complaints and refunds are handled in accordance with applicable law. Refunds are generally made through the original payment method unless another lawful arrangement is agreed.
3. These terms do not limit statutory consumer rights.
